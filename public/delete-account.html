Account & Medication Data Deletion

App: DawaSnap AI (Package: in.dawasnap.app) • Web Domain: https://dawasnap.vercel.app

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, Indian DPDP Act 2023, and global consumer privacy laws, users can permanently and irreversibly delete their DawaSnap AI authentication account, medication inventory, dosage records, and push tokens.

What Happens Upon Deletion?

All personal health entries, dose logs, AI consultation chats, and Firebase Authentication credentials are immediately and permanently erased from operational systems. Local device image caches are cleared on your browser/app.
