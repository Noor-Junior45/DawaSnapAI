About DawaSnap AI

DawaSnap AI (also indexed as DawaLens AI) is a next-generation medication intelligence and pharmacy tracker. Built with camera OCR vision algorithms and clinical safety standards, DawaSnap AI identifies medications, tracks expiry timelines, detects drug-drug interactions, and maintains a secure digital medicine vault for patients, families, and pharmacy managers.
