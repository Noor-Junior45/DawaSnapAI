Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and official answers regarding DawaSnap AI, smart medicine scanning, drug-drug interaction alerts, privacy safeguards, and multi-domain access.

What is DawaSnap AI (also called DawaLens AI)?

DawaSnap AI (and its registered alternative search term DawaLens AI) is an AI-powered medication scanner and inventory tracker. It utilizes camera OCR and Gemini vision AI to identify prescription and OTC medicines, log ingredients, monitor expiration dates, and alert users against dangerous drug interactions.

Which domains belong to DawaSnap AI?

The primary production web application is hosted at noorpos.in. Verified Google Cloud Run and Vercel edge mirrors include dawasnap.cloud.run, dawalens.cloud.run, dawasnap.vercel.app, and dawalens.vercel.app.

How does the camera OCR medicine scanner work?

Hold your smartphone camera or webcam up to any pill bottle, tablet blister pack, carton, or doctor prescription. The system instantly detects the medication name, strength/dosage (e.g. 500mg), manufacturer, and expiration date.

Does DawaSnap AI send alerts before medicines expire?

Yes. The app provides automated alerts at 30 days, 7 days, and 0 days prior to expiration via web push notifications, native Android notifications, and optional scheduled daily email alerts.

Is my medical and prescription data kept private?

Absolutely. We enforce a zero-telemetry architecture complying with GDPR, DPDP Act 2023, and Google Play Data Safety policies. All PII is removed before AI model calls, and users can export or permanently delete their account and records at any time.
